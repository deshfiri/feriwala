Feriwala ERP: Complete Project Requirements and Technical Specification
1. Project Overview
Feriwala will be a centralized ERP-based Wholesale and Dropshipping Management System.
The main Feriwala website will not operate as a public Product Storefront. Public visitors will not be able to browse Products, add Products to a cart, or complete a wholesale Checkout from the public website.
The public-facing Feriwala website will contain an Admin-managed Dynamic Landing Page with platform information, services, Package details, benefits, FAQs, Registration, Login, and other relevant content.
All operational activities will take place inside the authenticated Feriwala ERP Panel, including:
Product browsing
Dropshipping Product selection
Bulk Wholesale Purchasing
Package Management
KYC Management
Dedicated Website Management
Order Management
Inventory
Wallet and Money Management
Commissions
Direct Referrals
Withdrawals
Payments
Notifications
Reports
Eligible Packages may include Dedicated Customer-facing E-commerce Websites. These Partner Websites will operate as public Product Storefronts and will be connected to the Feriwala ERP through secure APIs and Webhooks.
The system will have a Single Account structure. Every user will register, update KYC, select a Package, pay the combined Registration Fee and Package Fee, complete administrative verification, and activate the same Account.
After activation, the user may use the same Account and ERP Panel for:
Dropshipping only
Bulk Wholesale Purchasing only
Both Dropshipping and Bulk Wholesale Purchasing
No separate Customer Account, Partner Account, or Account Conversion process will exist.
2. Required Technology Stack

The final system must follow a modular architecture so that its core business modules, Payment Gateways, SMS Providers, Courier Providers, and Website integrations can be extended without rewriting the entire application.

44. Mandatory Business Rules
The following Business Rules are mandatory:
Feriwala will operate as an ERP-based Wholesale and Dropshipping Management System.
The main public Website will have a Dynamic Landing Page and will not have a public Feriwala Product Storefront.
Wholesale Product browsing and purchasing will take place inside the authenticated ERP.
Every user will have only one Feriwala Account.
There will be no separate Customer or Partner Account.
The user must submit KYC after Registration.
The user must select a Package.
Registration Fee and Package Fee will be calculated and paid together.
Account activation will require Payment verification, KYC approval and Administrative approval.
The same Active Account can use Dropshipping, Wholesale Purchasing or both.
The system will support N number of configurable Packages.
Eligible Packages may include Dedicated Customer-facing E-commerce Websites.
Only the Admin or an Authorized User can create Products.
Users can only select Products from the Central Product Catalog.
Dedicated Websites will connect through secure APIs and Webhooks.
Feriwala will operate as a Centralized Advanced Order Management System.
Users can only access their own operational and Financial Data.
Every Active Account will have a Wallet and immutable Financial Ledger.
The Admin may configure required deposits and minimum Wallet balances.
Low Wallet balance may restrict Account or Website services.
The Direct Referral System will remain Single-Level and will not be MLM.
Active users can refer an unlimited number of people directly.
Both Referrer and new user may receive configurable rewards.
The Admin can configure Default Withdrawal Rules.
The Admin can configure user-specific Withdrawal Rules.
User-specific Withdrawal Rules will override corresponding Default Rules.
Every sensitive action must be recorded in the Audit Log.
Dashboard notifications and configurable SMS notifications are mandatory.
The ERP must have a clear, smooth, responsive and custom SaaS-style UI.
The interface must not look like a generic template or AI-generated Dashboard.
