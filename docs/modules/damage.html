Damage module

Damage cases (D35): a finding that delivered or returned goods are damaged, who bears the loss, what it costs, and what happens to the item. Returns and exchanges share a separate case workflow in the Order module; this page covers damages only.

Purpose

A Partner reports a delivered item damaged (the item may be sent back, or stay with the customer), or staff open a damage from a returned line during return inspection. Staff inspect, decide who bears the loss (Banij or the Partner), approve an amount, and post it. The Partner may then ask for the damaged item to be delivered to them. D35 is the authority (requirements/04-decisions.md); see Cases for the unified workspace and the return/exchange side.

Rule (D35). Nothing moves before inspection: no stock, charge or ledger entry until a person has inspected and the responsibility is decided. Only finalize moves money. A completed case is never reopened; cost is corrected by a compensating entry (reversal row plus a new charge), not by a status.

Key terms

Case / damage
One OrderDamage, reference prefix from ReferencePrefix::Damage.
Source
return_inspection (found on returned goods) or delivered_report (delivered product reported).
Parcel expected / in custody
Whether the item is coming back to Banij, and whether Banij now holds it (held_bucket). A customer-retained item has no parcel, no warehouse, no stock movement and can only be "damaged".
Disposition
What inspection decided for units: restock (to available), damaged (to damaged), quarantine (stay in returned).
Bearer / responsible party
partner or banij (ReturnParty). Customer fault is the Partner's to settle; Banij creates no debt for the customer.
Valuation
The maximum loss: the order line's frozen suggested selling price times damaged quantity.
Outstanding
A Partner-borne charge the wallet could not cover; owed, never a negative balance.
Dispatch
Delivery of the damaged item to the Partner who asked and paid.

Every action locks the case (lockForUpdate), returns the existing result if its step already happened, and writes an audit entry after commit (module damage).

Stock effects

  • Intake: sold to returned (only for a delivered report with a parcel).
  • Inspection: held bucket to the disposition bucket (restock to available, damaged to damaged, quarantine stays returned). Skipped when already in that bucket.
  • Dispatch: damaged to nowhere.
  • MoveDamageStock picks the Partner's own stock (PartnerStock, type adjustment) if the order line was reserved from it (D34), else central stock in the case's warehouse (needsAWarehouse if none). Key: order-damage:<public id>:<purpose>. Supplier-backed lines are refused.

Money

  • Banij bears: a DamageCharge with status recorded; no wallet movement, no platform wallet.
  • Partner bears: WalletService::debit (platform credit first, then withdrawable) with ledger type DamageLossDebit (delivery: DamageDeliveryDebit); status posted. If the wallet cannot cover it: outstanding.
  • Charge key: damage-charge:<public id>:<kind>:r<revision>; reversal key reversal:<charge key>. The reversal credits the typed balances exactly as taken (platform credit back as platform credit), each pointing at the ledger entry it corrects.

Permissions & scoping

Staff: damage.view, inspect, assign_liability, finalize, dispatch (plus view_self and request_delivery exist in the matrix as names for Partner verbs but are not used to grant). Typical roles: Order Manager and Admin view+inspect; Inventory and Fulfillment Manager view+inspect+dispatch; Finance Manager view+assign_liability+finalize. Separate verbs are intentional so the person who judges the goods does not decide or post the money.

Partners are never given damage.*: Gate::before refuses them to business identities (ReturnCasePolicy::isCaseAdministrationAbility). DamagePolicy instead scopes by account: ownedBy() requires the user's account to equal the case's account and the account role to hold ViewOrders; requestDelivery additionally requires the case to be Partner-borne and the role to hold UpdateAccount. MyDamages lists only cases the Partner bears the loss for; DamagePayload exposes no other Partner's data, internal notes, staff names, stock figures or valuation workings. Another Partner's case is a 404.

Money, ledger & audit effects

  • Money fields: valuation_unit_price, max_recoverable_value, approved_loss_amount, charge amount, dispatch delivery_charge, all Money.
  • Wallet ledger types: DamageLossDebit, DamageDeliveryDebit; reversals credit with ReturnAdjustment (correctsLedgerEntryId). Ledger entries are never edited.
  • Audit actions (module damage): damage.received, .inspection_started, .inspected, .liability_approved, .responsibility_changed, .finalized, .rejected, .delivery_requested, .dispatched, .received_by_partner; opening is audited by ReportDamage::auditOpened.
  • Staff are notified when a Partner reports (StaffQueue::DamageCase); Partner and next actor are notified on each move.

Configuration

The reporting window is the return window from the return terms (ReturnEligibility::windowClosesAt, delivered date plus the configured days). Delivery charges use the delivery-settings rules. No damage-specific env or config keys were found.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

  • A case "begins as the Partner's" in D35 text, but in code the responsible party is null until ApproveDamageLiability sets it; the first responsibility row has previous_party = null.
  • Valuation uses only line snapshots: suggested unit price, else platform-retained share, else none (maximum zero; write-off only). Never the Partner's store price or today's price.
  • Damaged units are never sellable afterwards, whatever the status; dispatch removes them from the damaged bucket only.
  • Responsibility cannot change once dispatch has been requested (the Partner paid for delivery based on it).
  • Supplier-backed lines cannot be reported (supplierBacked); the report screen says so up front.
  • Repeat calls are safe: each action returns the first result; unique keys settle races.

How to…

  1. Add a damage cause or condition: add the case to DamageCause/DamageCondition, widen the CHECK in a new migration, add statusLabelGroup translations (damage_cause/damage_condition) in en and bn, and the select options on the inspection and report screens.
  2. Add a status: edit DamageStatus::transitionsTo(), the helper predicates, the status CHECK, the history public_note keys in lang/*/damages.php, and the action that performs the move.
  3. Change how loss is valued: edit DamageValuation; remember the value is frozen onto the case at opening and the approval check compares against it.
  4. Add a charge kind: ReturnChargeKind case plus ledger type, widen order_damage_charges_kind_known.
