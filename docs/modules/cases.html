Cases module

The shared read side and evidence handling for returns, exchanges and damages (D35). app/Domain/Cases does not own the case workflows themselves: those live in app/Domain/Order (returns and exchanges) and app/Domain/Damage. It owns the evidence files, the "what is the next step" rule, and the queries that give staff and Partners one consistent view of any kind of case.

Purpose

A case is a return, an exchange or a damage. Returns and exchanges share the table order_returns (case_type = return | exchange, workflow_version = 2); a damage is its own record in order_damages. This module gives them one workspace: a unified staff queue, one timeline shape, one financial summary, one link builder, one evidence mechanism, and one definition of the next action and the permission it needs.

Users: platform staff (queue, case workspace, adding evidence), Partners (their own Returns & Exchanges list and detail, drafts and evidence on their own requests) and the system. Implements D35 and spec §18.2, §19.1, §26.3, §31.3. The actions that move a case (approve, inspect, settle, finalize) are documented on Order and Damage; notifications on Notification.

Key terms

Case
A return, an exchange or a damage. A "kind" string is return, exchange or damage.
Workflow version
order_returns.workflow_version: 1 for a return placed before D35 (finishes on the old screens with its old rules), 2 for the case workflow. CaseNextAction returns null for a version-1 return.
Draft evidence
A Partner's attachment while still writing a request: belongs to the order and uploader, not on any case, removable.
Submitted evidence
Evidence attached to a case. History: never deleted, never moved.
Responsible party / bearer
Who bears a cost, Partner or Banij. A case begins as the Partner's and only staff, with a reason, can say Banij.
Next action
The one step a case is waiting for, with the permission it needs.

Database constraints and triggers: status, uploader and purpose value CHECKs; a row belongs to at most one case; a row is on a case exactly when it is submitted; only a Partner drafts; a locked-columns trigger on identity columns; and feriwala_case_evidence_is_history() refusing deletion of submitted rows and changes to their status, case links or submit time. The migration 2026_11_18_100000 replaces the old column lock with one narrow, one-way guard for account erasure: display_name may only become [redacted], note may only become null or [redacted], neither can change again once redacted, and the file may be detached once. Nothing else is unlocked.

The cases themselves: see Order (order_returns, order_return_items, order_return_charges, responsibility changes, history) and Damage (order_damages and related tables). Earlier reference-only evidence on order_returns.evidence and order_damages.evidence is left as written and shown through EvidencePayload::legacy().

States & lifecycle

Evidence has two states, draft → submitted (one way). The case statuses are owned elsewhere; this module only reads them.

Return and exchange (ReturnStatus, version 2)

requested -> approved -> waiting_for_parcel -> parcel_received -> under_inspection
          -> inspection_approved -> settlement_pending -> completed
early exits: rejected (refusing the request), inspection_rejected (refusing the goods),
             cancelled (only while nothing has come back)

Approving writes approved and waiting_for_parcel as two history rows so no stage is skipped. A refused case is asked for again as a new one. Nothing moves before inspection approves the goods: no stock, refund, charge or ledger entry.

Damage (DamageStatus, per D35)

reported → (awaiting_receipt | received | under_inspection) → received → under_inspection → liability_review → approved → financial_posting_pending → finalized → dispatch_requested → dispatched → received_by_partner, with rejected from any stage before the decision. An inspection that finds nothing damaged ends the case with no loss. Case values used by CaseNextAction are listed below; the enum itself is on the Damage page.

Evidence upload flow

  1. The file goes through StoreManagedFile into the case-evidence area as a private file. The type is read from the file's own bytes (JPG, PNG, WebP, PDF per config('case_evidence.mime_types')), never the browser's claim. The size limit is the smaller of case_evidence.max_bytes and the server's upload_max_filesize/post_max_size.
  2. A UnacceptableFile becomes CaseEvidenceRefused::tooLarge or typeNotAccepted.
  3. The row is written with a cleaned display name and a note truncated to 500 characters.
  4. Serving: CaseEvidenceController::show() is reached only through a signed, short-lived link (cases.evidence.show, signed middleware, case_evidence.link_minutes). It authorizes view per request, reads the bytes off the disk, 404s for an erased file, and answers with no-store, nosniff, noindex and a locked-down CSP. A signature only proves the ERP issued the link, not that the holder may use it.

Permissions & scoping

ModuleActions in the catalogue (PermissionCatalogue)
returnview, create, review, receive, inspect, refund, export
exchangeview, create, review (its parcel, inspection and settlement are the return's permissions)
damageview, inspect, assign_liability, finalize, dispatch, view_self, request_delivery

One verb per job, so the person who decides who bears a loss cannot post it unless they also hold the right to post.

  • A Partner never holds these. The ordered Gate::before refuses every return., exchange. and damage. ability to a business identity. A Partner reaches cases through their own account (a query scope, not a permission), needs the right to read orders to read a case and the right to manage the store to ask for one. Another Partner's case, damage, order or evidence is a 404.
  • A Partner can request a return or exchange, report a damage, withdraw an unactioned request, add evidence to their own request, ask for a held damaged item to be delivered, and mark it received. They cannot say who is responsible, send an amount, change a status or finalize.
  • Evidence policy (CaseEvidencePolicy): a draft is its uploader's alone (not colleagues, not staff). Submitted evidence is openable by someone in the owning account who holds the account permission ViewOrders, or by staff holding damage.view (damage) or exchange.view/return.view (by case type). Delete is the uploader's, draft only. The check runs against the signed-in person on every request.
  • Staff queue visibility: a kind appears only with its view permission; badge counts never reveal how many cases of a hidden kind exist.

Money, ledger & audit effects

This module computes and displays money; it does not post it. Posting is in the Order module (PostReturnCharge, ReverseReturnCharge, StartReturnSettlement, ChangeReturnResponsibility) and the Damage module. The rules, from D35:

ChargePartner bearsBanij bears
Original deliveryHistory; never charged again-
Return chargeHalf the original delivery charge (rounded half away from zero to the poisha, e.g. 75.00 gives 37.50), debited from the wallet at settlementRecorded in accounting only
Exchange deliveryOne new deliveryAccounting only
Damage lossUp to snapshotted suggested selling price x damaged quantityAccounting only
Damaged-item deliveryAlways the Partner's (they ask for it)Never
  • A debit goes through WalletService (platform credit first, then withdrawable), writes an immutable ledger entry in the same transaction, and never forces a balance negative: an uncoverable charge is an outstanding liability collected when the money is there. A reversal credits exactly the kinds of money the debit took, via a new credit pointing at the entry it answers.
  • Idempotency: every financial effect has a key naming the case, kind and attribution revision, unique on the charge row, wallet transaction and ledger entry. Changing responsibility reverses a charge and posts it again to the new party: one compensating pair per charge, never an edit.
  • A refund is opened only by settlement after inspection approved, worked out from the order's immutable snapshots plus staff-approved adjustments (with a reason), never from a screen's figure. A dearer exchange's extra amount is recorded (who, how), never taken by the system.
  • Audit: evidence uploads audit with case reference and purpose only. The case-moving actions write their own audit rows (modules return, exchange, damage).

Rules, invariants & gotchas

Cross-links go through CaseLinks. Every link between an Order, case, Exchange Order and neighbouring exchanges is built by CaseLinks (url null when the reader may not open it). Never hand-build these URLs in a payload (.ai/rules/cases.md).

case_evidence is immutable by trigger, with one narrow exception. Migration 2026_11_18: display_name to [redacted], note to null or [redacted], and the file detached once with file_erased_at. Nothing else may be unlocked for erasure.

Nothing moves before inspection. No stock, refund, charge or ledger entry until a person has inspected and approved the goods (for a damage, until it is inspected and responsibility decided). A request, an approval and a parcel arriving are only statuses.

  • Sent evidence cannot be removed by anyone. A mistaken upload is answered with a new file and a note.
  • Buttons are not the boundary. CaseNextAction and the payloads decide what to show; each action re-checks permission and stage.
  • No customer data on lists. Queue rows, notifications and audit rows carry references and public ids, never names, numbers, addresses, evidence names or paths.
  • Case notifications come from the models' moveTo(), after commit, through AnnounceCaseEvent. Never add a second sender in an action.
  • Not supported, and says so: damage or return stock movement for a Supplier-supplied line (the report action refuses; staff see a warning); email, SMS or push for cases (in-app only); notifications go to the account owner only.
  • An earlier return (workflow_version 1) is not in CaseNextAction and finishes on the legacy desk.

How to...

Add a new evidence purpose

  1. Add the case to EvidencePurpose (and to forStaff() if staff add it) and a label under the case_evidence_purpose group in lang/*.
  2. Widen the case_evidence_purpose_known CHECK in a new migration.
  3. Map it to a permission in StoreCaseEvidence::requiredPermission().
  4. Add tests (AdminCaseWorkspaceTest style) for allowed and refused staff.

Add a stage's next action

  1. Add the status to the match in CaseNextAction::for() with the key and the permission it needs.
  2. Add the label under cases.next.{key} in lang/en/cases.php and lang/bn/cases.php (CaseTranslationsTest guards the pair).
  3. Add the route and action (see Order/Damage); the action must re-check permission and stage.

Show a new field on the staff or Partner case page

  1. Add it to AdminCasePayload or PartnerCasePayload. Money comes from CaseFinancials as the server's own Money output; do not compute in React.
  2. Check the Partner payload for leakage (staff note, warehouse, Banij amount) and add a test in PartnerCaseScreensTest.

Change the evidence limits

  1. Set the env variables or edit config/case_evidence.php. The effective size limit is also bounded by PHP's upload settings.
