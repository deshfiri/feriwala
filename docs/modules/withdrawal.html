Withdrawals

A Client or Partner business account asks to move withdrawable money out of its wallet to a saved payout method. Staff review, approve, process and mark it paid with the external reference. The money is reserved at request time and captured or released exactly once.

Purpose

Implements §27 and D25 for BusinessAccount owners. Suppliers have a parallel but separate model (SupplierWithdrawal, in the Supplier domain, out of scope here). The business requests; platform staff decide and pay outside the system, then record the bank or mobile-wallet reference.

  • Client / Partner: requests and tracks its own withdrawals.
  • Staff: a queue under Admin, with separated duties (below).
  • Admin with withdrawal.manage_settings: sets minimum and maximum.

Key terms

Claim
The wallet reservation (wallet_transactions row, type withdrawal_debit) named by wallet_transaction_id. Pending while reserved, settled when paid, cancelled when released.
Payout snapshot
A frozen copy of the payout method (type, label, masked number, bank, branch) taken at request time. Editing or archiving the method later never changes it.
External reference
The proof of payment staff enter when marking paid.

Actions & workflows

Request (RequestAccountWithdrawal)

  1. Account must be activated; KycRestrictions::blocksWithdrawals() must be false (a new request only; one already in flight is never cancelled by a KYC consequence, see .ai/rules/withdrawal.md).
  2. The payout method must belong to this account and be active.
  3. Amount at least the minimum and at most the maximum (if any).
  4. An existing withdrawal with the same idempotency key is returned.
  5. Transaction: lock the wallet; refuse if a Partner-gateway receivable hold is live (PartnerCollectionHolds::firstLive, D36, taken under the same wallet lock a collection uses); refuse if the amount exceeds availableForWithdrawal(); WalletService::reserve() with withdrawal_debit (withdrawable money only, key account-withdrawal-reserve:{key}); create the row with the payout snapshot; first status-history row (source account); audit account_withdrawal.requested.
  6. After commit, announce to the staff queue (StaffQueue::AccountWithdrawal). A unique-key race returns the winner's row.

Review and payment

  1. AdvanceAccountWithdrawalStatus moves requested to under_review, under_review to approved, approved to processing. No money moves. Audit account_withdrawal.{status}.
  2. RejectOrFailAccountWithdrawal (to rejected or failed, reason required): lock the row, check the transition, WalletService::release() the claim, store failure_reason for failed, write history with the reason, audit, and notify the owner (AccountWithdrawalStatusChanged).
  3. PayAccountWithdrawal: lock, check canTransitionTo(paid), WalletService::capture() the claim (total and reserved fall together, one ledger entry, key claim key + :capture), set processed and paid times and the external reference, write history, audit account_withdrawal.paid (sensitive), notify.

The claim's own state machine is what makes release and capture happen once: a repeat is refused before the wallet is touched.

No fees. I found no withdrawal fee in app/Domain/Withdrawal; the amount requested is the amount reserved and captured. Limits are the only rule beyond the balance.

Decision columns. decided_by, decided_at and decision_note exist and are read by screens and the mobile resource, but I found no action that writes them. Who decided is recoverable from the status history and audit log.

Limits (AccountWithdrawalLimits)

  • Minimum: the account's override, else setting account.withdrawal_minimum, else 500.00; never below the floor 1.00.
  • Maximum: the account's override, else setting account.withdrawal_maximum; none by default; capped at 1000000000.00 when set.
  • SetAccountWithdrawalLimits writes the default or an override and audits account.withdrawal_limits_default_set / account.withdrawal_limit_override_set (sensitive). Authorisation is the controller's job.

Permissions & scoping

Separation of duties through three permissions on the shared withdrawal module: a holder of approve may advance and process; reject may reject and mark failed; only release_payment may mark paid. manage_settings is held by no seeded role (the WithdrawalApprover role deliberately lacks it); reachable by Super Admin. Staff viewAny/view never fall back to "has a business account": otherwise any account owner would reach the staff queue.

Self-scoping is the query: Erp\AccountWithdrawalController reads only the signed-in account's rows and never asks the staff policy. The idempotency key stored is account-withdrawal:{account id}:{client token}, so one account's token cannot collide with another's.

Money, ledger & audit effects

  • Request: no ledger entry (a reservation). Pay: one withdrawal_debit entry on the withdrawable kind. Reject or fail: no ledger entry, only the release event.
  • Audit actions: account_withdrawal.requested, .under_review, .approved, .processing, .rejected, .failed, .paid, plus the limit actions above. Module withdrawal.
  • Invariant: the reserved amount equals the withdrawal amount until it is captured or released; the amount is positive and in the wallet's currency.

Configuration

Settings account.withdrawal_minimum, account.withdrawal_maximum (decimal, group account), and the per-account override columns. Constants in AccountWithdrawalLimits: DEFAULT_MINIMUM 500.00, FLOOR_MINIMUM 1.00, CEILING_MAXIMUM 1000000000.00.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

  • The generic WalletService::reserve() checks usableBalance(); the withdrawal action checks the stricter availableForWithdrawal() itself, because the required deposit stays out of reach even when it may cover service charges.
  • A withdrawal only ever claims withdrawable money; platform credit cannot be withdrawn until converted (Wallet).
  • The idempotency key here is the durable, unique-index kind, not the cache-backed IdempotencyService. The client must send a stable token per submission.
  • The Admin approve button performs two transitions when the request is still requested.
  • Payout details are never read from the method after request time; always use payout_snapshot.
  • Money is paid outside the system. Marking paid records a fact and captures the reservation; nothing here calls a payout API.

How to…

Add a withdrawal status

  1. Add the case and its transitionsTo(), tone(), label (lang withdrawal.php) in AccountWithdrawalStatus.
  2. Extend the account_withdrawals_status_known check in a new migration (and the history table check if the source list changes).
  3. Decide whether it holds the reservation (holdsReservation()) and whether leaving it must release or capture the claim.
  4. Add the Action and the admin route and policy method; update admin/account-withdrawals/show.tsx.

Add a withdrawal fee

  1. Raise it for approval first (payment direction and ledger are architecture-locked).
  2. It needs its own ledger type, a second capture entry in PayAccountWithdrawal, and a limit check against amount plus fee in RequestAccountWithdrawal.

Give one account a higher limit

Admin, Withdrawal limits, account override (withdrawal.manage_settings); this sets the two override columns and audits.
