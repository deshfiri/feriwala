Wholesale module

The partner's wholesale cart and checkout: server-priced lines, quantity pricing, coupon, delivery, tax, an address, and a confirmation that is bound to an exact fingerprint of what was priced. Placing the order itself lives in the Order module.

Purpose

A business account with the wholesale facility buys central-catalogue products in bulk for itself or its own resale stock (§14, D34). app/Domain/Wholesale owns the cart, the pricing of cart and checkout, and the confirmation step. Order\Actions\PlaceWholesaleOrder turns a confirmed checkout into an order, a payment and stock reservations; payment and order tracking are covered in Order and Billing. Every figure is computed on the server on every read; the browser sends intent (units, quantity, payment method, fingerprint), never a price, total or tax.

Key terms

Cart
One per user (unique user_id), tied to the business account they work in. Holds intent only.
Cart line
A stockable unit (product, or one variation) and a quantity.
Unit price seen
The price last shown for a line, kept only to flag a later change; never charged.
Checkout fingerprint
SHA-256 over currency, lines, subtotal, coupon, discount, delivery, tax charges, total and both address snapshots. A confirmation stands only while the freshly priced checkout has the same fingerprint.
Confirmation
Fields on the cart: payment_method, confirmed_at, confirmed_fingerprint, confirmed_total.
Facility
The package feature wholesale_enabled; without it the account has no wholesale.

No status enum. The cart is a working document; the order it produces has its own states (Order).

States & lifecycle

Cart phases: empty, building, ready for checkout (CartQuote::isReadyForCheckout(): not empty, no line problems, no unaccepted price changes), confirmed (fingerprint stored), then ordered. After an order: paid (or paid and held for review) clears the lines, coupon and confirmation (CloseOrderedCart::afterPayment); cancelled unpaid keeps the lines and clears the confirmation (afterCancellation). Both act only if the cart still holds the confirmation the order was placed from.

Line problems (CartLineQuote constants): unavailable, choose_variation, variation_unavailable, below_minimum, above_maximum, insufficient_stock. A line with a problem is excluded from the subtotal.

Actions & workflows

Cart

  1. OpenCart::forUser() finds or creates the person's cart; a cart for a different account is deleted rather than repriced; a creation race is settled by the unique index.
  2. SetCartLine sets (not adds) a quantity, so retries cannot double an order. Refused in this order: not eligible on the wholesale channel (ProductEligibility::isEligible(..., SalesChannel::Wholesale)), variation required or inactive, below minimum / above maximum order quantity, and, when StockEnforcement::enforced(), not enough stock (StockAvailability for this account, including its own allocation). It remembers the tier price at that quantity as unit_price_seen. The cart is locked; the cap is PriceCart::MAXIMUM_LINES = 100. The cart does not reserve stock.
  3. AcceptCartPrices copies each changed current price into unit_price_seen, clearing the "price changed" notice.

Pricing (every read)

  1. PriceCart::quote() re-checks each line: eligibility, variation, quantity bounds, stock, and WholesalePriceResolver::unitPrice() at the line quantity. Line total = unit price times quantity; priceChanged when it differs from the seen price.
  2. PriceCheckout::quote() adds: coupon (CouponValidator::validateForWholesale, rechecked, a bad code simply stops discounting); delivery from FeeRuleResolver::wholesaleDelivery() for the account's package (zero if nothing is sold); discount shared across lines proportionally with Money::allocate(); tax per line on the post-discount net and on the delivery charge via TaxEngine; total = subtotal - discount + delivery + tax added (tax-inclusive amounts are shown, never added twice). Billing and shipping come from the user's current addresses.

Checkout and order

  1. Addresses: SaveCheckoutAddress (billing or shipping, edited in place, user row locked) or UseSavedAddressForCheckout (one of the account's own active shared addresses becomes both; the order takes its own snapshot).
  2. ApplyCheckoutCoupon stores only the coupon's code on the cart when accepted; nothing is redeemed.
  3. ConfirmCheckout::handle($user, $account, $method, $fingerprintSeen), under a cart lock: reprice; require ready cart and both addresses; the method must be the wallet (covered by balance) or an available gateway for the currency; hash_equals on the fingerprint else CheckoutRefused::changed(); then store the confirmation. Same fingerprint and method again is a no-op. withdraw() clears it. It takes no payment, creates no order, reserves nothing.
  4. PlaceWholesaleOrder::handle() (Order module): refuses if KYC re-verification blocks new orders; in one transaction under a cart lock verifies the confirmation and fingerprint again, records one payment (PaymentPurpose::WholesaleOrder, key wholesale-payment:...), creates the order (source = erp_wholesale, payment_pending, full payment model, snapshot of lines), reserves every line's stock in product order through StockReservations (supplier-backed lines allocate from a supplier offer), sets the payment deadline SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_SECONDS = 60 before the reservations expire, and holds the coupon. Idempotent per confirmation (key wholesale-order:<hash of cart, fingerprint, confirmed_at>); a stock shortfall is OrderRefused::stockUnavailable() when enforcement is on.

Console: catalog:verify-wholesale-pricing

Read-only deployment guard (VerifyWholesalePricingCommand). Run with --snapshot=<file> before a pricing migration and --against=<file> after. The snapshot stores the exact Postgres decimal text of base_cost, wholesale_price and the three selling-price bounds for products, and base_cost, wholesale_price for variants, plus SKUs/ids, counts, nulls, zeros, scales and a checksum. The comparison exits non-zero on any changed, nulled, zeroed or re-scaled value, missing or duplicated row. Snapshots are refused inside the repository, public/ or storage/, written mode 0600, and values are printed only with --show-values. --allow-explicit-percentage (development only) tolerates a deliberate switch to percentage pricing.

Permissions & scoping

Access is by account, not by staff permission: the facility gate above plus business.activated; the cart is reached only through the signed-in user and their account (OpenCart), so another user's cart cannot be named (§31.3). Orders are looked up through the account (404 otherwise). The permission matrix has a wholesale module (view, create, edit, export); I found no code that checks it. Staff wholesale-order administration is under the Order module.

Money, ledger & audit effects

  • All figures are Money (flat Taka decimal strings, bcmath). Discount is allocated with Money::allocate() so shares sum exactly; weights use the currency's smallest unit via Currency::smallestUnit(), not a bare multiplication by 100.
  • The cart/checkout write no ledger entries. The payment (and a wallet debit if the wallet is the method) and invoice are created and settled in the Order/Billing/Wallet modules. A paid order credits Partner stock through ReceivePartnerPurchase (Inventory).
  • Idempotency: order key per confirmation, payment key derived from it, reservation references <order reference>-L<n>.

Configuration

Settings row inventory.stock_blocks_orders (does stock refuse cart lines and orders); reservation windows inventory.reservation_online_minutes (payment deadline); fee rules and tax rules and coupons come from their own modules; gateway availability from PaymentGatewayManager.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

Rule. The browser never sends a price. Send a fingerprint; the server reprices and refuses a mismatch. Keep CheckoutQuote::fingerprint() in step with anything that changes what is charged, or a confirmation may survive a change it should not.

  • Cart stock checks are advisory; the binding check is the reservation at order placement.
  • With stock enforcement off, placement tolerates a failed reservation (the line gets no reservation).
  • Prices on a line with an unaccepted change block checkout until AcceptCartPrices.
  • Every account pays the whole total (D34); the old delivery-only payment for Non-Conditional accounts is no longer created.
  • Reservations are taken in product/variation order across checkouts to avoid lock-order deadlocks.
  • A line whose product has since gone ineligible shows no price (unitPrice = null).

How to…

  1. Add a charge to checkout: compute it in PriceCheckout::quote(), add it to CheckoutQuote and its fingerprint(), add a QuoteLine in WholesalePaymentQuote::fromCheckout(), snapshot it in PlaceWholesaleOrder::createOrder(), and update the screens and mobile resources.
  2. Add a cart line rule: add a constant to CartLineQuote, check it in both PriceCart::line() and SetCartLine (and a CartRefused factory plus lang strings).
  3. Check prices survive a migration: run the verify command before and after (above).
