Order module

The record of every sale in Feriwala: ERP wholesale purchases, storefront (Store) orders, and exchange replacements. It owns the order and its immutable line snapshots, four independent status axes, allocation of lines to stock sources, the money consequences of delivery (supplier payables, Partner profit), unpaid-order cancellation, platform receivables for Partner-gateway orders, and returns and exchanges. This is the largest module; read it before touching anything that sells, ships or refunds.

Rules that bind this module. An order and its lines are snapshots: the database refuses to change or delete them. Order status moves only through moveTo() (which calls transitionTo()) along the OrderStatus map, and the database holds the same map. Money is flat Taka held in Money; nothing here converts through poisha. Every financial consequence runs inside a transaction under row locks, is idempotent, and leaves a ledger or audit record.

Purpose

The module answers "what was sold, to whom, for how much, where it came from, how far it has got, and who is owed what because of it". It implements specification §14 (wholesale checkout), §17 (website orders), §18 (orders, sources, statuses, history), §19.1 and §20 (reservation and fulfilment), §21 (shipping and couriers), §26.3 (refunds of returns), §28 (cash on delivery and settlement) and §31.3 (self-scoping).

There are three kinds of order in everyday use, distinguished by orders.source (the OrderSource enum):

Orders are used by Partners (see and cancel their own), platform staff (view, move, allocate, ship, return) and the system (payment settlement, schedulers, courier webhooks). Customers never touch the ERP; they act through the Storefront API (see Storefront API).

Decisions implemented: D8 (couriers), D12 (merchant of record; retail margin is a calculation base, not a separate money path), D13 (wholesale shares the fulfilment pipeline), D25 (Supplier allocation), D34 (wholesale always paid in full; storefront selling is the Non-Conditional route), D35 (returns, exchanges, damages), D36 (Partner-owned gateways, Model B, platform receivable). Code comments also cite "D-new"; that is the rule D34 replaced (see Gotchas).

Key terms

Store Order
An order with source = website: a customer bought from a Partner's storefront. Staff and Partners see it on the "store orders" screens; its status moves only through named actions (StoreOrderAction).
Wholesale order
An order with source = erp_wholesale: a Partner buying from Feriwala. wholesale_payment_model = full is stamped on every order placed since D34.
Exchange Order
An order with source = exchange, created with an exchange case. Carries original_order_id and exchange_return_id. Display number EX-{original number}, then -2, -3.
Conditional / Non-Conditional
The AccountType of the placing account, snapshotted on the order as orders.account_type. A Non-Conditional Partner sells storefront products without buying them first; Feriwala keeps its share from the proceeds.
Four axes
Order status, plus fulfillment_status, delivery_status and courier_status. Each is its own state machine with its own history table.
Allocation
A frozen decision to fulfil part of one order line from one source: a warehouse, or a Supplier offer (OrderItemAllocation).
Dispatch gate
DispatchGate: delivery and courier may not start until fulfilment is Ready for dispatch (non-Store orders).
Proceeds settlement
OrderProceedsSettlement: per line, the two independent facts (delivered, money collected) that must both be true before a Non-Conditional Partner's earning is credited.
Recoverable amount
OrderItem::recoverableAmount(): what Feriwala keeps from a line's proceeds before the Partner earns anything.
Collection channel
CollectionChannel: cod, platform_gateway or partner_gateway_external. Says how a line's money reached whoever holds it.
Platform receivable
What Feriwala is owed on a Partner-gateway Store Order (D36), frozen at placement and collected from the Partner's wallet.
Case
A return, exchange or damage. Returns and exchanges are rows of order_returns; see Cases and Damage.

Data model

Models live in app/Domain/Order/Models/. Money columns use MoneyCast and carry a currency_code column; all are exact decimal Taka. Public ids are ULIDs through HasPublicId; admin and ERP URLs bind on public_id, never on the numeric id.

All status enums implement TransitionableState. Nothing assigns a status attribute directly. The order model exposes one move method per axis, each writing the change and its history row together and stating who did it and why:

How the axes relate

  • Fulfilment gates dispatch. DispatchGate: for every order except a Store Order, the first move off not_shipped / unassigned, and any re-assignment, needs fulfillment_status to be ready_for_dispatch or fulfilled. Hold and cancel are always allowed, and a track already under way is never blocked. The gate is consulted by the manual status actions, shipment creation and the screen's offered moves.
  • Store Orders have their own gate. AdvanceOrderFulfilmentStatus refuses a Store Order outright ("use the named actions"). AdvanceOrderDeliveryStatus and AdvanceOrderCourierStatus refuse a Store Order until StoreOrderStep::hasDispatched(), i.e. status is shipped, in_transit, delivery_failed, delivered, completed or a return stage.
  • A shipment drives the axes. RecordManualCourierStatusUpdate moves the shipment, mirrors it onto the order's courier axis, and onto the delivery axis where there is an unambiguous equivalent (picked_up → shipped, in_transit → in_transit, delivered → delivered, failed_delivery → failed_delivery, cancelled → cancelled). returned_to_origin and assigned/pickup_requested deliberately have no delivery mapping.
  • Delivery rolls up. RollUpOrderStatus runs inside every fulfilment and delivery move (see Gotchas for exactly what it does).

Flow 1: ERP wholesale order

  1. The Partner confirms a checkout (the Wholesale module's cart and PriceCheckout produce a fingerprinted quote). PlaceWholesaleOrder::handle() first asks KycRestrictions::blocksNewOrders(): an outstanding KYC re-verification can stop new orders (§7.4), checked in the action rather than by hiding a button.
  2. Under a row lock on the cart, in one transaction: the checkout is priced again and must be ready, addressed, payable through a switched-on gateway (or the wallet), and carry the same fingerprint the Partner confirmed. A total that is not positive is refused ("nothing to pay").
  3. WholesalePaymentQuote::fromCheckout() builds the payment (D34: the whole server-calculated total, every account). For a wallet payment, WalletPaymentMethod::canCover() refuses early. RecordPaymentFromQuote writes the payment with purpose WholesaleOrder and an idempotency key derived from the cart confirmation.
  4. The order is created: source = erp_wholesale, status = payment_pending, wholesale_payment_model = full, account_type snapshot, customer and address snapshots, every Money total from the quote, cod_fee zero.
  5. Stock is reserved line by line, in a fixed product order so two orders never lock the same stock in opposite orders. AllocateSupplierOrderLine (Supplier module) chooses a Supplier's preferred offer where the variation is Supplier-sourced (D25), otherwise central stock; the reservation kind is OnlinePayment. When stock is not enforced, a refused reservation is tolerated; when it is, the order is refused.
  6. Each line is snapshotted (writeLines()): price, line discount capped at the subtotal, tax at the rate in force, reservation id, and the Supplier snapshot. A SupplierPayable is accrued for each Supplier-backed line.
  7. The payment gets its gateway, mode (sandbox or live, stamped now and never recalculated, §26.4) and an expires_at one minute before the first reservation lapses (SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_SECONDS = 60). A coupon use is held against the payment.
  8. recordPlacement() writes the first history row (source checkout, public note orders.notes.placed).
  9. Idempotency. The key is a SHA-256 of cart public id, confirmed fingerprint and confirmation time. The same confirmation submitted twice returns the first order; the database refuses a second order for the key, a second payment_pending order for the cart, or a second order for the payment.
  10. The Partner pays (InitiateOrderPayment opens a gateway session; ChangeOrderPaymentMethod can switch gateway; PayOrderFromWallet pays from the wallet). Settlement in the Billing module calls ConfirmOrderPayment.
  11. ConfirmOrderPayment, with the order then the payment locked: every line's reservation must match its line (reference <order>-L<line>, quantity) and still be active; reservations are committed (Partner-sourced ones are only held until dispatch); status → paid with paid_at; ReceivePartnerPurchase gives the Partner the units they bought (D34); IssueInvoice issues the invoice (P4-11, only now); the cart is emptied; supplier payables record the payment fact; the online collection fact is recorded in its own savepoint so a bookkeeping failure never undoes a real payment.
  12. If the stock cannot be committed (missing, mismatched, ended, or a lock timeout): everything rolls back and the order moves to on_hold with held_at and hold_reason, supplier payables are frozen (HoldSupplierPayable), a critical log is written on the payment channel and a payment log row order_held. The money is real; a person must find stock or refund. No invoice is issued for a held order.
  13. Fulfilment proceeds on the axes (Flow 5). Delivery runs Flow 6.

Flow 2: how a storefront order enters

A customer of a Partner's separately installed storefront never touches the ERP. The storefront calls the signed Storefront API (POST storefront/v1/orders, scope orders:write, with the idempotent middleware). PlaceWebsiteOrder::handle():

  1. WebsiteCheckoutRules is asserted first: the website is accepting orders (an outstanding KYC re-verification answers exactly as a paused shop does), the payment request is legal (a payment the storefront calls paid is refused outright, D12), and no coupon is carried.
  2. A distributed lock on website-order:{website id}:{lower-cased storefront reference} serializes duplicates. The same reference with the same request returns the existing order ([order, false]); the same reference with a different request is WebsiteOrderRefused::duplicate; a reused idempotency key is keyReused.
  3. PriceWebsiteOrder prices the cart itself. The storefront's claimed unit prices and totals must match exactly (WebsiteOrderQuote::matches()); any difference is refused with the authoritative figures. Nothing the storefront sends is trusted but what it bought.
  4. In one transaction: the website's customer is found or recorded by mobile number (RecordWebsiteCustomer); for COD the shop's COD limit is checked; one payment is recorded (purpose WebsiteOrder); the order is written; a Non-Conditional account's selling prices are re-checked against the platform price rules (assertPricedWithinRules: the suggested price is always acceptable); stock is reserved for the website's owner.
  5. Stock. reserveFromPartner() draws on the Partner's own units (D34). Supplier offers are never drawn on for a Store Order. A line the owner has too few of refuses the order, except a Non-Conditional account, which sells without having bought: that line is filled from central stock and the line records product_cost (the wholesale price that account would have paid) and platform_retained_amount (suggested selling price times quantity). Every line freezes suggested_unit_price (what a damaged unit is worth at most, D35).
  6. Initial status. COD: customer_verification_pending. Online: payment_pending. For online payment ChooseWebsiteOrderGateway decides, once, between Model A (Feriwala's gateway) and Model B (the Partner's own, D36) and freezes gateway, gateway_mode, gateway_ownership, funds_destination and the configuration version on the payment; a fallback from the Partner's gateway to Feriwala's is logged with a fixed-list reason. For COD, the payment records no gateway (Order::isCashOnDelivery()).
  7. The payment window closes one minute before the first reservation lapses. The placement history row has source storefront.
  8. After the transaction: a COD customer is sent the code (SendCodConfirmationCode, never allowed to fail a taken order) unless the shop or the platform asks for no code (CheckoutVerification), in which case ConfirmCodOrder::withoutCode() confirms at once and the history says so honestly. The customer's number is never recorded as verified in that case.

Flow 3: customer confirmation of a COD order

  1. The storefront posts orders/{order}/confirmation/code (resend) and orders/{order}/confirmation (the code). SendCodConfirmationCode uses the same VerificationCodes as mobile verification: hashed, short life, a budget of wrong guesses, a resend cooldown, scoped to the order. It goes by SMS (event cod_confirmation, see Notification) and is never returned to the storefront.
  2. ConfirmCodOrder::handle() takes a lock cod-confirm:{order id}, re-reads the order under a row lock, and is safe to repeat: an order already confirmed is answered as it stands. Only customer_verification_pending is moved. A closed window (the stock's own deadline) is confirmationExpired. A wrong, spent or never-issued code gets one answer, so guessing reveals nothing about whether an order exists.
  3. Confirming commits what the order was holding (Partner-sourced reservations are held until dispatch; others are committed) and moves to confirmed. It does not make the order paid: the money is collected on delivery and settled through §28, so no invoice is issued.
  4. ExpireUnconfirmedCodOrders (scheduled every minute) cancels COD orders nobody confirmed in time, with reason confirmation_expired.

Flow 4: Store Order workflow, dispatch and cancellation

  • AdvanceStoreOrder re-reads the order under a row lock. If the order is already at the target status it answers [order, false]: no second history row, no second stock movement, no second storefront announcement. Otherwise the action must be legal from the current status or StoreOrderRefused::wrongStep.
  • Stock boundaries. confirm calls holdUntilDispatch() on each Partner-sourced reservation (staff confirmation proves the order is real, so the short window ends). Processing, packing and packed leave the hold. dispatch is the one boundary that consumes stock: each Partner-sourced reservation is committed exactly once, in the same transaction as the status move; a reservation that ended any other way refuses the dispatch rather than shipping goods nobody holds.
  • CancelStoreOrder: only before dispatch (new ... ready_for_pickup), always with a reason. Stock is released once from the reservation still held, including any replacement from order_hold_reservations. An unpaid payment is closed; a settled one sends the order to refund_pending and the existing refund process takes over (this action never refunds). A Partner may cancel only while the order is still Pending (new, pending_confirmation, customer_verification_pending, payment_pending); once it is being processed, cancelling needs staff. A dispatched order is never cancelled here: it follows the return, failed-delivery and return-to-origin flows.
  • CancelUnpaidOrder (wholesale and website): takes the lock payment:settle:{payment id} (the same lock settlement takes, so a cancel and a gateway confirmation queue rather than race), moves only payment_pending or customer_verification_pending orders, never one whose payment has settled, refuses a person cancelling while the gateway is still confirming, closes the open payment, releases stock once, moves to cancelled, withdraws the cart confirmation, cancels pending supplier payables and releases the coupon hold. A payment the gateway confirms after cancellation goes to reconciliation, never to the cancelled order.
  • ExpireUnpaidOrders (every minute) handles three kinds: paid-but-waiting orders (confirmed now, or held), orders whose payment has closed (cancelled), and orders whose payment window closed (cancelled, stock returned; late money is reconciled).

Flow 5: allocation of lines to sources

Allocation is staff-driven and applies to orders with Supplier or warehouse sourcing (not to Store Orders, which reserve Partner stock). The platform never chooses: AllocationSourceCandidates reports what every source holds and what it would earn (Supplier rate, warehouse cost, platform rate, margin), and AllocateOrderLineSource::handle() commits to the one a person picked.

  1. A reason is required. The call runs under a per-line distributed lock order-line:allocate:{item id} (30 s TTL, wait up to 10 s) and a transaction.
  2. Locks: the line, its order, and the line's active allocations. replacingAllocationId names the one allocation being handed to a new source; omitted, the call either re-confirms an already-active exact source (returns it untouched, so a double click cannot reserve or owe twice) or adds a new split for the still-unallocated units. It never implicitly replaces anything.
  3. Reallocation guard. Replacing an allocation is only allowed before picking or dispatch: canReallocate() is an exhaustive match over fulfilment and delivery statuses (a new status added later fails to compile until someone states what it means). Past that point only an override works, and only when the caller has already checked OrderPolicy::overrideFulfilmentState(); the override (who, when, why) is recorded on the allocation.
  4. Quantity. The default is whatever is not already covered by another active allocation. A request of zero, or more than remains, is refused.
  5. The chosen source is re-read live inside the lock via AllocationSourceCandidates. The panel is a view and can be minutes old; this is the decision. A source that has become unavailable is refused with its reason, never silently swapped.
  6. If replacing, standDown() releases the previous reservation (or cancels its fulfilment commitment, for a non-ready-stock offer), cancels the previous pending payable, and marks the old allocation superseded.
  7. commitTo(): a Supplier offer row is locked first (taking the strongest lock first, in the same order everywhere, is what prevents a lock-upgrade deadlock that a concurrency test caught). A ready_stock offer or a warehouse takes a reservation (reserveFromSupplier, or reserve against the exact ordered product or the linked stock item); on_demand and pre_order offers hold no physical stock and instead record a capacity commitment (RecordSupplierFulfilmentCommitment; FulfilmentCapacityExhausted becomes a refusal).
  8. The OrderItemAllocation is written with the offer's current price version, an idempotency key order-line-allocation:{line public id}:{ulid}, and expected_margin recomputed against the quantity actually committed. If the partial unique index refuses (another request won), the reservation is given straight back and the call fails with "allocated by someone else a moment ago".
  9. A Supplier source raises the pending payable through AccrueSupplierPayable::forAllocation() (triggering event staff_allocation or reallocation). A warehouse line never creates a payable.
  10. Audit: order_line.allocated or order_line.reallocated, with before/after, the reason, and module order. Staff figures (unit cost, margin) are in the audit row; they never reach a Partner payload.

Flow 6: delivery and its financial consequences

Reaching Delivered on the delivery axis (by hand through AdvanceOrderDeliveryStatus, or through a shipment via RecordManualCourierStatusUpdate, which a Steadfast webhook or poll also uses) runs, in one transaction under the order's row lock, in this order:

  1. moveDeliveryTo() writes the move and its history.
  2. RollUpOrderStatus: if the order's own status can legally move to delivered, MarkPartnerStockSold first moves a Store or Exchange Order's units from the processing bucket to sold (keyed delivered-line:{item public id}, so repeats move nothing), then the status moves to delivered. If fulfilment is fulfilled, delivery is delivered, the payment is settled and the move is legal, status moves to completed.
  3. SynchronizeSupplierPayablesWithDelivery: delivered marks every supplier payable delivered (EvaluateSupplierPayableEligibility); failed_delivery, cancelled and returned cancel the still-Pending ones.
  4. SynchronizeDeliverySuccessFeesWithDelivery: charges the Delivery Success Fee against every supplier payable on the order, identically for both account types and a no-op for a line with no Supplier.
  5. SynchronizeOrderProceedsWithDelivery: first asks SynchronizeOnlineCollectionWithPayment again (repairs a collection fact an earlier failed attempt left unwritten), then records "delivered" on every line where defersProductCost() is true.
  6. Audit row order.delivery_status.advanced.

Flow 7: proceeds settlement and the Partner's earning

A Partner's earning on a Non-Conditional line becomes real only when two independent facts are both recorded on its OrderProceedsSettlement: the order reached Delivered, and the money was actually collected. "Delivered" is not "cash collected"; crediting a wallet from delivery alone is exactly the mistake this shape prevents. It mirrors the supplier-payable shape on the other side.

  • Which lines. OrderItem::defersProductCost(): false for an exchange line; true when platform_retained_amount or product_cost is frozen, or for a legacy Non-Conditional wholesale line that is not on the full payment model.
  • Recoverable amount. recoverableAmount(): zero when the order's product cost was paid in full (Order::productCostWasPaid()); otherwise platform_retained_amount (or, for older lines, product_cost) plus the line's tax and tax_included; a legacy wholesale line keeps its whole line_total. The tax collected on a line is never the Partner's.
  • Three ways money is collected (CollectionChannel). cod: staff confirm the cash (RecordCodCollection, route admin.orders.lines.cod-collection.store; refused for a line already collected through a gateway). platform_gateway: a verified payment to Feriwala's gateway; the line's share is its own line_total (delivery charge and COD fee never become profit). partner_gateway_external: a verified payment to the Partner's own gateway; recorded for reporting only, the Partner already holds that money.
  • Settling. EvaluateOrderProceedsEligibility::apply() is idempotent and safe under races: it creates the row if needed (the unique order_item_id index settles a delivered-versus-collected race; the loser retries), locks it, takes each fact once, and only when both are in does settle() run. The same channel again changes nothing; a different channel for an already-collected line throws a LogicException.
  • Earning. earning = collected - recovered_amount, measured against what was actually collected, never the declared estimate. A negative result is a shortfall: flagged_for_review = true, earning recorded as zero, an audit row marked sensitive, and no credit. It is never silently zeroed.
  • Credit. A positive earning is credited with WalletService::credit() as LedgerTransactionType::SalesCredit, source order_proceeds_settlement, idempotency key order-proceeds-settlement:{settlement public id}; the wallet transaction id is stored on the row. A Partner-gateway line is marked eligible with an earning of zero and an audit note ("none: the Partner collected this payment directly"); Partner margin there is order-level and reported from the frozen receivable.
  • Reversal on return. ReverseOrderProceedsSettlement::forReturn(), per return line and idempotent on proceeds-reversal:{return item public id}: it computes the part of the credited earning belonging to all units returned so far (Money::allocate(), the last unit takes the rest) minus what was already taken back, and debits the wallet with a new ReturnAdjustment entry pointing at the entry it answers, out of the same kind of money it was credited as. It never fails the return: if the wallet cannot cover it (the Partner already withdrew), the settlement is flagged for review and a critical log is written on the wallet channel.

Flow 8: Partner-owned gateway receivable (D36, Model B)

  1. At placement ChooseWebsiteOrderGateway grants Model B only when everything holds: Admin allows Partner gateways and this provider; the account is Non-Conditional; every line is filled from central stock and has a defined frozen amount; the storefront did not request a different provider by name; the receivable snapshot was written completely. Otherwise the order is Feriwala's, with a reason from a fixed list. The snapshot is written last, in a savepoint. The choice is final.
  2. FreezePlatformReceivable writes the receivable: the sum of OrderItem::recoverableAmount() over the lines, plus the frozen delivery charge and delivery tax as separate component lines (PlatformReceivableCalculator). It excludes the selling price, Partner margin, supplier fee and payables, provider fees and any commission.
  3. When the Partner's gateway payment is verified, OpenReceivableCollection opens one collection (unique per order) with a deadline (deadline_hours, default 24 h, from SetPartnerCollectionDeadline, frozen on the row) counted from verification, not from when the job ran. ConfirmOrderPayment deliberately leaves a Partner-owned payment alone: verification proves the customer paid the Partner, not that Feriwala was paid.
  4. ReleaseCollectableReceivables is the single entry point (payment verified, wallet credited, the scheduled sweep partner-receivable-collection-sweep, and the staff "retry" action). One account at a time under the account's collection lock, oldest verified first; each receivable needs its whole amount, and nothing newer is debited while an older one still waits for money.
  5. CollectPartnerReceivable, in one transaction: lock order, collection, payment and wallet; answer a non-collectable one as it stands; check the payment is still the verified Partner-owned one bound to this order and the receivable equals the frozen figure; secure stock for every line (SecureReceivableStock keeps an active reservation or sets stock aside again under a new reference, linked through order_hold_reservations); then, only if the wallet can cover the whole amount, debit it (platform credit first, then withdrawable; one ledger entry per balance type), commit stock, mark the collection collected and move the order to paid.
  6. Never partial, never negative. If the wallet is short the wallet is untouched, the collection is wallet_insufficient and the order on_hold with reason partner_wallet_insufficient. If stock cannot be secured, nothing is debited and the collection becomes reservation_recovery_required for staff. Past the deadline the stock is released and the collection becomes deadline_expired_refund_required (reason wallet_deadline_expired_refund_required): nothing claims the customer was refunded and nothing is cancelled automatically; the Partner refunds through their own gateway or funds the wallet and retries.

Flow 9: returns and exchanges (summary)

A return or exchange is a case. The case's workspace, evidence and notifications are on the Cases page; damages on Damage. In this module:

  1. RequestOrderReturn opens a case from a Partner's panel, the storefront (POST storefront/v1/orders/{order}/return-requests, scope returns:write) or staff. It is a request only: nothing moves. The case begins as the Partner's responsibility, never Banij's. The return charge (half the order's frozen delivery charge, rounded half away from zero to the poisha, bcmath, from a locked column) is snapshotted now. What is asked for is checked against the order under a lock, counting what earlier cases already claim. An idempotency key makes a retry the same case.
  2. DecideOrderReturn (reason always required) approves per-line quantities, never more than asked, writing approved then waiting_for_parcel as two history rows. ReceiveReturnParcel records only who received a box and when. StartReturnInspection names who has the goods.
  3. FinalizeReturnInspection is the one decision that lets stock and money move: it counts each line, gives it a disposition (back on sale, damaged, held), and approves via RestoreReturnedStock (once per line, through the stock ledgers, into the stock the unit left: Partner units, a Supplier offer's stock with its payable reversed for the same quantity, or the warehouse). Rejecting restocks nothing and gives the claimed units back. A damaged line is the finding a damage case is opened from.
  4. StartReturnSettlement, under one lock on the case, posts the return charge (PostReturnCharge: Banij-borne is accounting only; Partner-borne is a WalletService debit, or an outstanding liability when the wallet cannot cover it, never a negative balance), opens the refund through RefundOrderReturn::openFor() and the existing refund machinery (D17), and moves to settlement_pending. A cash-on-delivery refund is never sent to a gateway: it is marked for manual review and recorded by SettleReturnRefundManually (permission payment.reverse_transaction, confirmed password, two-factor). SyncReturnRefund carries the refund's fate back. CompleteReturnCase closes it when the customer's side is done.
  5. Exchanges. CreateExchangeOrder (inside the case's transaction) creates the replacement Order (source = exchange, pending_confirmation, no stock held). ConfirmExchangeOrder reserves and commits its stock, Partner's own units first and central stock only for a Non-Conditional account, in the same transaction as the inspection approval, so an approval is refused if the replacement cannot be sent. CancelExchangeOrder calls it off if the case ends without goods coming back, unless it has already shipped. AllocateExchangeNumber allocates EX- numbers under a lock on the original order.
  6. Storefront and notifications. AnnounceReturnStatus writes return.status_changed (and refund.completed once money has actually gone back) as a webhook delivery in the same transaction. Return, exchange and damage notifications to staff and Partners come from AnnounceCaseEvent, which the models' moveTo() call.

Money, ledger & audit effects

Money fields

All order money is Money (flat Taka, exact decimal string, bcmath), with currency_code. Order totals: subtotal, discount, delivery, tax (added), tax_included, cod_fee, total. Supplier-rate columns use MoneyCast::class.':supplier_currency_code'. A client-submitted total is never trusted: a storefront that claims a price differing from the server's by any amount is refused. Allocation, refund and profit reversal use Money::allocate() so parts reconcile exactly.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

Snapshots are law. An order, its lines, its allocations' decision columns, its status histories, and the receivable are written once. The model throws and the database refuses. Never "fix" an order by updating a column: add a new row (a history entry, an adjustment, a reversal, a new case).

Never assign a status. Use moveTo() / moveFulfilmentTo() / moveDeliveryTo() / moveCourierTo() (which call transitionTo()) and always write the history. The database enforces the same map from the seeded transition tables, so a bypass fails at the database.

RollUpOrderStatus does less than its docblock says. Its docblock describes a best-effort sync for picking, packing and shipped states, but the code only handles two things: delivery Delivered → status delivered (plus MarkPartnerStockSold), and fulfilled+delivered+paid → completed. It is silently skipped when the move is not legal (OrderLifecycleStateMachineTest asserts this: status stays new). In the code read, no action moves a non-Store order's status from paid to processing/packing/shipped; only the three axes advance for wholesale orders, and paid cannot legally move straight to delivered. Treat the axes as the source of truth for wholesale fulfilment progress, and verify before relying on orders.status for a wholesale order after payment.

Store Orders and the dispatch gate. DispatchGate does not apply to Store Orders (they have their own workflow), and the manual fulfilment action refuses them. Do not add a status-picker for Store Orders; add a StoreOrderAction.

"D-new" and D34. Code comments in proceeds, allocation and delivery-fee classes cite "D-new". That is the earlier rule (a Non-Conditional wholesale order paid delivery only, with product cost recovered from COD) which D34 replaced. Orders placed before D34 have no wholesale_payment_model, are deliberately not backfilled, and are handled by what they stored (account-type snapshot, resale_amount, payment amount), never by the Partner's current type. defersProductCost() is true for a legacy Non-Conditional line and false for a full order.

Legacy allocation by link. ConfirmProductSourceLink and ProductSourceLink are legacy: allocation offers only sources for the exact ordered product and variation, and confirmed links never produce a candidate. order_items.sourcing_* columns are never written now. Do not build on either.

Commission is a calculation base, not a money path (D12). There is no commission engine in this module. For a Non-Conditional storefront line, the Partner's profit is the store selling price minus the frozen platform_retained_amount snapshot (D34), measured by OrderProceedsSettlement and credited through the one wallet path. Partner margin on a Partner-gateway order is "externally verified gross customer payment minus frozen receivable" and is reporting only (D36): it is never credited to the wallet. How a Conditional account's storefront proceeds are credited is not part of the order code read for this page (no settlement row is written for lines that do not defer product cost); consult the Commission and Wallet pages.

  • One live allocation per line, one live payable per line. Enforced by partial unique indexes, not by action discipline. Split allocation (several active allocations on one line, each for part of the quantity, added by migration 2026_10_21_100000_allow_split_order_item_allocations) changes the "one" to one per slot; read that migration and SplitAllocationTest before changing it.
  • The panel is advisory. The allocation panel is a view; AllocateOrderLineSource re-reads availability under lock and refuses stale choices. Never reuse a candidate list as a decision.
  • Lock order matters. The supplier offer is locked before the allocation insert, because an insert takes an implicit share lock on the offer and a later lock-for-update deadlocks (found by OrderLineAllocationConcurrencyTest). Reservations are taken in product/variant order so orders for the same products queue instead of deadlocking.
  • Settlement lock. Cancelling an unpaid order and settling its payment both take payment:settle:{payment id}. Do not cancel an order without it.
  • Partner-sourced stock is held until dispatch. holdUntilDispatch() on confirmation or payment; dispatch (AdvanceStoreOrder) commits. Non-Partner-sourced reservations commit at confirmation or payment.
  • Payment is the payment's answer. An order keeps no payment status; read payment->status, or OrderPaymentState::of($payment). COD is "payment recorded with no gateway" (isCashOnDelivery()).
  • Held, not paid. A settled payment whose stock cannot be committed puts the order on_hold for a human. Do not "fix" it by forcing paid.
  • Notifications for cases come from the models' moveTo(), after commit (.ai/rules/cases.md and notification.md). Never add a second sender in an action. Cross-links between an Order, its case and Exchange Order are built only by CaseLinks (a url is null when the reader may not open it).
  • An exchange line is not a sale. defersProductCost() is false for it; it has no payment of its own and creates no second profit, commission or supplier payable.
  • Public ids only. URLs use public_id (ULID). A webhook or API payload is a hint (identifiers, the move, when), never the order itself.
  • Stale comments. The early orders migration still mentions _minor column names; later migrations moved to flat Taka (D26). Do not infer a minor-unit convention from them. The repository guard test fails the build if new production code reintroduces one.

How to...

Add a new order status

  1. Add the case and its transitionsTo() entries (and tone()) in app/Domain/Order/Enums/OrderStatus.php. Update every other state's list that may lead to it.
  2. Add a migration that inserts the row into order_statuses and the edges into order_status_transitions (precedent: 2026_11_10_100000_add_store_order_workflow_transitions.php). The enum's docblock says a test fails when the enum and the tables disagree; run tests/Feature/Orders/OrderStatusTest.php and OrderStatusTransitionTest.php.
  3. Add the label in lang/en and lang/bn under the group named by statusLabelGroup() (order).
  4. If it is a Store Order stage, map it in StoreOrderStep::for(); decide whether StoreOrderStep::hasDispatched() and CancelStoreOrder's lists include it.
  5. Add the action that makes the move, using $order->moveTo(...) inside a transaction under lockForUpdate(), with a StatusChange and a publicNote lang key. Add a test for the move and for the refusal from a wrong status.

Add a new step to the Store Order workflow

  1. Add a case to StoreOrderAction with allowedFrom(), to() and a label in lang/*/store_orders.php (store_orders.actions.*).
  2. Make sure the underlying OrderStatus transition is legal (see above).
  3. Decide its stock boundary in AdvanceStoreOrder: holds persist until dispatch; only dispatch consumes.
  4. Add a store_orders.notes.* public note, and cover idempotency (repeat answers [order, false]) in StoreOrderWorkflowTest.

Add a field to an order or line snapshot

  1. New migration adding the column. For money use decimal(19,2) with a CHECK, plus the currency_code that already exists on the row.
  2. Add it to the model's casts() (MoneyCast for money) and the @property docblock. If it is a snapshot, add it to the migration's locked-columns trigger so it cannot change after insert.
  3. Write it in PlaceWholesaleOrder / PlaceWebsiteOrder (createOrder() or the line builders). Never accept it from the client; take it from the server-side quote.
  4. If it is a staff-only figure, keep it out of every Partner and Storefront payload (check WebsiteOrderPayload, WholesaleOrderTracking, StoreOrderTimeline).

Add a new order source

  1. Add the case to OrderSource with a label().
  2. Add a migration widening the orders_source_known CHECK (D35's exchange did this in 2026_11_16_110000_add_exchange_orders.php).
  3. Decide what each source-aware class does: DispatchGate::appliesTo(), AdvanceOrderFulfilmentStatus, MarkPartnerStockSold, SteadfastOrderRequestBuilder, OrderItem::defersProductCost(). Search for OrderSource::.

Change what a delivered order triggers

  1. Add a Synchronize...WithDelivery action beside the existing three, taking (Order, OrderDeliveryStatus) and returning early unless the target is the one you care about.
  2. Inject it in AdvanceOrderDeliveryStatus and call it in the same transaction, after RollUpOrderStatus. It must be idempotent: a webhook and a poll can both deliver the same news.
  3. Any wallet effect goes through WalletService with an idempotency key derived from a stable public id, and writes an audit row.

Change who can allocate

  1. Edit OrderPolicy::viewAllocationSources() and allocateSource() together: both must cover every figure the panel shows, and the second must hold independently of the first.
  2. Update OrderAllocationPermissionsTest and OrderPolicyTest.
