Referral (Partner Network)

A multi-level referral hierarchy between business accounts, with versioned reward plans. A qualifying event (account activation after the verified activation payment) records one commission row per level; a scheduler pays each into the beneficiary's wallet after a holding period, and reversals are compensating ledger entries. The UI calls it the Partner Network.

Rule. Multi-level commission is switched off by default (setting referral.mlm_enabled). Switching it on and choosing the plan is the operator's decision, to be taken with legal advice (D24 compliance note). Nothing is calculated while it is off.

Purpose

Every active user receives a referral code. A new business can register with a referrer's code. When that business activates, the platform decides which ancestors, up to the plan's depth, earn a reward, and credits the new account's own joining reward. D24 (2026-09-19) supersedes the original single-level §25; D14 stands (the joining reward is the new user's referral reward). Spec §25, §25.4.1, §25.5.

  • Client / Partner sees their code, link, direct referrals and own earnings only: no downline beyond level 1, no one else's KYC or wallet.
  • Staff view platform records, configure plans, reverse commissions, and attach a referrer.
  • System calculates at activation and pays on a schedule.

Key terms

Referral code
8 characters from 23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ (no I, L, O, U, 0, 1), random, stored on users.referral_code. Normalised by uppercasing and stripping non-alphanumerics.
Referrer / referred
Business accounts. At most one direct referrer per account.
Level
Steps up the chain: 1 is the direct referrer. Level 0 is the new account's joining reward. Levels are never renumbered when one is skipped.
Plan version
A dated, immutable configuration: trigger, base, depth, rules per level, joining reward, holding days, qualifying account states. Changing anything is a new version.
Qualifying event
The thing that earns commission; recorded once per account ever.
Commission base
What a percentage is a percentage of: activation_fees (registration plus package fee), package_fee or registration_fee, from the payment's own allocations.
Outbox
The commission rows, written in the activation's own transaction and paid later.

Flow 1: registration

Fortify\CreateNewUser and CreateManagedAccount call AttachReferrer::atRegistration with the resolved referrer. ReferralCode::issueTo issues a code on first view of the referrals page if the person has none (and the business can transact). Staff can attach or correct a referrer with a reason, but only before a qualifying event has locked the link.

Flow 2: calculation at activation

  1. ActivateAccount calls forActivation() inside its own transaction.
  2. Stop (return null) if the switch is off, if the event already exists (returned as is: a retry or second worker pays nothing twice), or if no plan is in force. Plan: the account's package first, then the global one (ResolveReferralPlan; a package plan replaces the global plan entirely, not level by level).
  3. Use the latest paid activation payment. Stop if there is none or it is below minimum_qualifying_payment.
  4. Base from CommissionBaseCalculator: chosen allocations summed; any discount is shared across revenue lines in proportion and the chosen lines' share is rounded up, so the base rounds down; tax and deposit are never part of it.
  5. Ancestors to the plan's depth (recursive SQL; upward only). Each level is decided alone: no ancestor ends the chain (missing); a disabled level, unqualified account state, wrong package, or too few active direct referrals records skipped at that level while the levels above keep theirs. The rule gives fixed or base × bps ÷ 10 000 rounded toward zero, held to its cap and to what remains of the base (marked capped). Levels are processed from 1 up, then the joining reward, so the whole chain never exceeds the base.
  6. Write the event (savepoint, so a lost race on the unique index does not abort the activation), one commission per level with available_at = now + holding_days, lock the links the chain was read through (locked_at), audit referral.commissions_calculated, and after commit dispatch ReleaseReferralCommissions.

Flow 3: payment

  1. The job (right away) and the scheduled sweep (referral-commission-release, every five minutes) call ReleaseReferralCommission for pending rows whose available_at has passed (up to 500 per sweep).
  2. Lock the commission; skip unless still pending and due. A closed beneficiary cancels it (cause beneficiary_closed). A beneficiary whose status the plan does not qualify waits and is retried.
  3. WalletService::credit() on the beneficiary's wallet (OpenWallet creates one if missing) with type referral_reward_credit or joining_reward_credit and key referral-commission:{public id}. Both default to platform credit: spendable, not withdrawable.
  4. Set paid, paid_at, wallet_transaction_id; after commit notify the owner (ReferralCommissionPaid).

Flow 4: reversal

  1. Triggers: a settled refund of the qualifying payment (ProcessRefund calls ReverseQualifyingEvent::forRefund; any refund, partial included, reverses the whole event; it never fails the refund, and logs critical if it cannot), or staff (ReverseReferralByStaff, reason required).
  2. ReverseQualifyingEvent marks the event reversed and reverses each pending or paid commission in level order through ReverseReferralCommission.
  3. Pending: cancelled, no ledger entry. Paid: a referral_reward_reversal debit pointing at the original entry (corrects_ledger_entry_id), key referral-commission-reversal:{public id}, taken from the kind of money it was paid as. If the wallet cannot cover it the commission becomes reversal_owed; the sweep retries (recovered count).
  4. Audit referral.commission_reversed (sensitive) and referral.event_reversed.

Permissions & scoping

Permission module referral: view, create, edit, approve, reject, reverse_transaction, export, manage_settings, view_settings. The ones used by controllers are listed above. An account sees only its own: ReferralRecords::earning() is "the part of a commission its beneficiary may see", and nothing in the ERP asks for a whole downline (ReferralHierarchy walks upward only; directReferrals() is level 1).

Money, ledger & audit effects

  • Money fields are Money via MoneyCast; plan percentages are integer basis points, never floats.
  • Ledger: referral_reward_credit, joining_reward_credit, referral_reward_reversal (see Wallet).
  • Audit: referral.mlm_switched, referral.plan_superseded, plan opened and closed, referral.referrer_attached, referral.commissions_calculated, referral.commission_reversed, referral.event_reversed.
  • Invariants: one event per account ever; commission terms never change (snapshot kept); the chain never pays more than the base; a reversal is only ever a compensating entry or a cancellation.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

  • A plan needs a rule for every level up to its depth or nothing is opened. Opening takes a distributed lock per scope (referral-plan-scope:{package id|global}); the version in force ends where the new one begins; a version scheduled at or after the new start makes the new one fail (planOverlaps).
  • A referrer is never changed silently, and never after a qualifying event has locked the link.
  • Only the activation trigger exists. The skip rules, the cap and holding period are all per plan version.
  • The beneficiary's qualification is checked twice: at calculation (level rules) and at payment (account status via the plan's flags).
  • Any refund, partial or not, of the qualifying payment reverses the whole chain (§25.5).
  • ReferralHierarchy::WHOLE_CHAIN is 100 000, matching the database's own cycle guard walk.

How to…

Add a trigger event (for example an order)

  1. Add a case to ReferralTrigger; teach ResolveReferralPlan callers and OpenReferralPlan (the draft) to use it.
  2. Write a calculator method beside forActivation that records the event for its own subject type, once.
  3. Extend the unique key and the event/commission triggers if the subject differs, and the reversal path for that payment.

Add a commission base

  1. Add the case and its allocations to CommissionBase; CommissionBaseCalculator needs no change.

Change rewards

Open a new plan version in Admin, Referral settings. Never edit a plan or a commission.
