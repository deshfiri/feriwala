Inventory module

Central warehouse stock, the stock ledger, reservations, per-account allocations, a Partner's own purchased stock, and the availability figures every website is told. Everything that changes a stock figure goes through one ledger service in one transaction, under a row lock.

Purpose

Spec §19 and §19.1 require stock that cannot be oversold, that has an explanation for every change, and that is shared consistently across all partner websites. This module holds three separate pools of units, each with the same discipline (a movement between buckets, written with the figures it explains):

  • Central stock (stock_items): Feriwala's warehouse stock, per warehouse and per stockable unit (the product, or each variation). Written only by platform staff and by order/return flows.
  • Partner stock (partner_stock_items, D34): the units a Partner bought through paid wholesale orders. A storefront sells from this; central stock is not read for it, except for a Non-Conditional account (below).
  • Supplier offer stock (owned by the Supplier module) which uses the same reservation lifecycle through StockReservations::reserveFromSupplier().

Used by admins (inventory managers), the system (reservation expiry, low-stock alerts, out-of-stock protection), partners (read-only allocated stock and My Products ownership), and the Storefront API (availability only). Implements §19, §19.1, contract §5.2 and §6.1.2 (reservations), D15 (warehouse order), D25 (supplier stock), D34 (partner-owned stock).

Rule. Nothing writes a stock bucket column except StockLedger (central) and PartnerStock (partner). Every change is a movement (units leave one bucket and arrive in another, or enter/leave stock entirely) written in the same transaction as the figures, with every bucket's figure before and after. Movements, adjustments and partner movements are append-only in the database. Corrections are new movements.

Key terms

Bucket
One of the states a unit can be in: available, reserved, processing, sold, returned, damaged, and (central only) allocated. Each is an integer column on the stock item named like the enum value.
Stockable unit
A product with no variations, or one variation of a product with variations. A product with variations is not itself a unit; its own SKU is omitted from availability answers.
Movement
A row in stock_movements (or partner_stock_movements): type, from bucket, to bucket, quantity, before and after figures, reason, actor, source, idempotency key.
Adjustment
A stock change made by hand (StockAdjustmentKind) with who and why; linked one-to-one to its movement.
Reservation
Units held for an order not yet confirmed, ending exactly once (committed, released or expired). Identified by a unique reference.
Allocation
Available stock set aside for one business account (P3-30). That account's own orders draw on it first; nobody else can.
Reservation kind
online_payment (default 15 minute window) or cod (default 24 hour confirmation window).
Non-Conditional account
An account type that sells without having bought stock; its storefront availability falls back to central stock when it owns no available unit.
Stock enforcement
The switch inventory.stock_blocks_orders. On (default): carts and orders beyond available stock are refused. Off: stock is counted but never blocks.

States & lifecycle

Stock buckets

A central unit's life: (arrives) to available; available to reserved when an order is placed; reserved to processing when the reservation is committed; processing to sold on delivery (Order flows); sold to returned on a return or damage intake; returned to available (restock) or damaged after inspection; damaged to available (repair) or out of stock (write-off, dispatch). available to allocated sets units aside for one account and back again.

Reserving stock for an order

  1. The order flow (e.g. PlaceWholesaleOrder, PlaceWebsiteOrder) calls StockReservations::reserve() (central), reserveFromSupplier() or reserveFromPartner() with a unique $reference.
  2. If the reference already exists, the existing reservation is returned, but only if it matches the same product, variant, quantity, kind and account; otherwise InventoryRefused::referenceInUse().
  3. Under the distributed lock inventory:reserve:<product>:<variant|product> (partner: inventory:reserve:partner:<account>:<product>:<variant>), it builds the candidate list: the ordering account's own allocation first (in warehouse order, only if it covers the whole quantity), then any one active warehouse whose free stock covers the whole quantity. Never a split across warehouses or between allocation and shared stock.
  4. For the first candidate that still holds enough after the row lock: allocate the reservation id from the sequence, StockLedger::move() available (or allocated) to reserved with type reservation, decrement the allocation, and create the active reservation with expires_at from ReservationWindows::expiryFor($kind).
  5. No candidate holds it: InventoryRefused::outOfStock($sku, $requested, $held). Callers decide whether StockEnforcement::enforced() turns that into a refusal.

Ending a reservation

  1. commit(), release() or expire() lock the reservation by inventory:reservation:<id> and in a transaction re-read it with lockForUpdate.
  2. Repeat of the same end state returns the row; another end state throws. An expire() of one whose expiry was extended meanwhile throws notYetExpired.
  3. Central: reserved to processing (commit), or back to available (release/expire) or back to allocated (and the allocation quantity restored) when it had drawn on an allocation. Supplier/partner sources are delegated to SupplierStockReservations / PartnerStockReservations.
  4. The reservation is moved with transitionTo() and stamped (committed_at, or released_at + reason + overridden_by).

holdUntilDispatch() pushes an active partner reservation's expiry out about 10 years once payment/confirmation proves the order real (D34), so the sweep never frees it. extend() keeps a reservation alive to a given time (D36 wallet deadline) and never shortens it.

What websites are told (availability)

  1. Storefront API GET inventory and inventory/{sku} use PartnerStockAvailability: the available bucket of the Partner's own row for that exact variation. Non-Conditional accounts fall back to central availability where they own none.
  2. StockAvailability (central) sums available across active warehouses plus the asking account's own allocation only. If any supplier offer exists for the unit, the answer comes from supplier stock instead and central stock is not consulted.
  3. The shape is exactly SKU, in_stock, quantity, updated_at: never warehouse, reservation detail or another account's allocation (contract §5.2). The figure is advisory; the reservation at order time is binding.
  4. SyncWebsiteInventory (scheduler, every five minutes) tells websites about changed stock using this same query.

Money, ledger & audit effects

  • The stock ledger is a quantity ledger, not a money ledger; no money fields. Nothing in this module posts a wallet or financial ledger entry.
  • Idempotency keys: central movements take MovementContext::idempotencyKey (unique); reservation reference (unique); partner purchase credits wholesale-line:<order item public id>; partner reservations partner-reservation:<reference>; damage flows order-damage:<damage public id>:<purpose>.
  • Audit actions (module inventory): inventory.stock_tracked, .stock_adjusted, .stock_deleted, .low_stock_threshold_set, .stock_allocated, .allocation_released (sensitive), .reservation_released_by_hand, .reservation_extended, .reservation_windows_set (sensitive), .stock_enforcement_switched, .warehouse_created, .warehouse_updated, .warehouse_default_changed. Automatic reservation moves and ledger movements are not individually audited; the movement row is their record.

Rules, invariants & gotchas

Never write a bucket column or a stock_* table directly. Use StockLedger::move() / PartnerStock::move(). A figure changed without a movement cannot be explained, and the CHECK/trigger layer (non-negative, allocation sums) will reject inconsistent writes.

  • Overselling prevention has three layers: the distributed lock (contention), the row lock + re-check inside the ledger (correctness), the CHECK constraint (backstop).
  • Lock order is item then allocation in every path (allocate, release, reserve, end). Keep it.
  • A reservation is one whole line from one source. No split across warehouses, no mixing allocation and shared stock.
  • Availability excludes inactive warehouses and everything but available (+ the asking account's own allocation). Switching a warehouse on/off therefore changes availability with no movement, which is why ManageWarehouses::update() triggers a stock response.
  • Out of stock is only automatic for tracked products, and only between active and out_of_stock. Nothing is auto-activated that a person took off sale.
  • Stock responses run after commit (afterCommit); failures are reported, never rethrown.
  • Reservation windows are stamped at reservation time. Changing a setting does not shorten an existing hold.
  • The default warehouse can never be inactive; make another warehouse default first.
  • New central items open at zero; stock arrives only by adjustment (receive), so every unit has a who/why.
  • Deleting a stock item with history is a soft delete and is only allowed once its product is trashed or force-deleted; a live product's stock row with history is refused.
  • A damaged unit never becomes sellable except by an inspector's explicit restock decision (see Damage). Supplier-backed order lines cannot be moved by the damage flow (DamageRefused::supplierBacked).
  • Raw-SQL stock queries bypass the product soft-delete scope; StockAvailability adds products.deleted_at IS NULL explicitly. Do the same in any new raw query.

How to…

Add a new stock adjustment kind

  1. Add the case to StockAdjustmentKind with source(), destination() and a label.
  2. Update the stock_adjustments_kind_known CHECK in a new migration.
  3. Add the label/option to lang/en/inventory.php and lang/bn/inventory.php and the dialog in resources/js/pages/admin/inventory/adjust-stock-dialog.tsx.
  4. Cover it in tests/Feature/Inventory/AdjustStockTest.php.

Add a new movement type

  1. Add the case to StockMovementType (or PartnerStockMovementType).
  2. Update stock_movements_type_known (or the partner equivalent) with a migration that re-states the full list (see the allocation migration for the pattern).
  3. Call StockLedger::move() with a MovementContext carrying reason, actor, sourceType/sourceId and an idempotency key if externally triggered.

Make an order flow consume stock

  1. Choose a unique, stable $reference per line (for example order reference plus line number).
  2. Reserve, in a deterministic product order (to avoid deadlocks), through StockReservations; handle InventoryRefused and LockTimeout; consult StockEnforcement::enforced().
  3. Commit on payment/confirmation, release on cancel; never touch buckets directly.

Change how long reservations hold

  1. Prefer the admin screen (admin.inventory.reservations.windows, needs inventory.approve).
  2. To change bounds or defaults edit the constants in ReservationWindows and the validation in SetReservationWindows. The storefront contract (§6.1.2) fixes 15 minutes and 24 hours as v1 defaults; do not change defaults without approval.
