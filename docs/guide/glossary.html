Glossary

Domain terms and project acronyms as the code, the specification (requirements.txt) and the decision log use them, in alphabetical order. Where a term has a module page it is linked.

Note. The product's brand name is Banij (APP_NAME, many comments and seeded emails), while the repository, spec and some logins say Feriwala. Both mean this system. "Partner" and "Client" both appear for the account that trades; the code's entity is BusinessAccount.

§ (section reference)
A numbered section of requirements.txt, the original specification, for example §12 (product restrictions), §23.2 (ledger), §31.3 (self-scoping), §36 (security). Code comments cite them. See Decisions.
Account Owner
The registered, activated user who owns a BusinessAccount. A user owns at most one (D23). Staff are members, not owners. See Account.
Action
A class in app/Domain/<Module>/Actions with one public handle(). Business logic lives in Actions; controllers validate, authorize, delegate and respond, and models hold relationships, casts and scopes only.
Activation
The move of a business account to Active. Automatic once KYC is approved, the exact payment settled and no hold exists (D27); manual otherwise. See Account.
Activation hold
Three columns on business_accounts (activation_hold_reason, activation_held_at, activation_held_by), all present or all null. It disables only the automatic activation path (D27).
Admin / Super Admin
Platform roles (admin, super_admin) among the twenty-one in PlatformRole. The Super Admin alone may clear the audit log (D33) and manage website integration credentials (D32). See Access.
Allocation (order item allocation)
A frozen decision about which source (warehouse stock or a Supplier offer) fulfils one order line, with its cost, selling price and margin locked by the database. Only workflow columns move. Table order_item_allocations. See Sourcing.
Audit log
The audit_logs table recording sensitive actions. UPDATE and TRUNCATE are refused; DELETE only through the guarded purge (D33), which leaves a permanent audit.logs_purged entry. See Audit.
Available to Spend
Wallet figure from usableBalance(): total less reserved, hold, pending, minimum balance and (where applicable) required deposit, floored at zero. Not the same as withdrawable. See Wallet.
Banij
The brand name of the product. Appears as APP_NAME, in the Banij ERP heading of CLAUDE.md, and in DNS verification (_banij-verify).
BDT / Taka
Bangladeshi Taka, the base and only operational currency in v1 (D4). Money is flat Taka: 100 means BDT 100 at every layer (D26).
BJ number (partner number)
A partner's identity number: BJ plus eleven digits, held in business_accounts.partner_number and assigned by HasPartnerNumber or the partners:assign-numbers command. See Commands.
Business Account
App\Domain\Account\Models\BusinessAccount, the commercial workspace with the 22 §5.3 statuses. Distinct from the User login identity (D23). Named so that "Account" alone is not read as "the thing I log into". See Account.
Case
A return, an exchange or a damage handled by the one case workflow (D35). Returns and exchanges share order_returns; a damage is order_damages. See Cases and Damage.
Central catalogue / Central Product
Feriwala's single product catalogue. Only admins create products; partners select from it and Suppliers propose listings against it (§12, D25). See Catalog.
Collection channel
How the money behind a Partner's proceeds was collected: cod, platform_gateway or partner_gateway_external (CollectionChannel, D36). A settlement row with no channel predates the field and is COD.
COD (cash on delivery)
Payment collected by the courier at delivery. Tracked separately from online payment, with its own confirmation, collection and reconciliation (§28). COD orders hold stock under a confirmation window. See Order.
Commission
A Partner's earning on a dropshipping sale, rule-driven and reversible (§22). Retail margin is one calculation base on the rule, not a separate money path (D12).
Commission base
What a rule calculates from. For referral plans: the package fee, the registration fee or both (CommissionBase), taken net of discount and excluding tax and deposits (D24). See Referral.
Conditional / Non-Conditional account
AccountType, set directly by an administrator. Under D34 both pay ERP wholesale in full; Non-Conditional Partners additionally sell Storefront Products with no upfront product cost, and only they are eligible for Model B gateways (D36). Older orders keep their original behaviour.
Contract (Storefront API contract)
requirements/05-storefront-api-contract.md, frozen at v1. Non-breaking additions may ship into v1; breaking changes need v2 and approval. See Storefront API.
Credential (website credential)
A storefront's signing identity: key id plus encrypted secret, scoped to one website, with scopes, rotation and revocation. The secret is shown once. See Website.
Currency code
The currency_code column that sits beside every money column (default BDT). Currency mismatches are rejected by Money.
D-number
A decision in requirements/04-decisions.md, D1 to D36. Note two decisions are headed D30 and a few code comments cite an old D29 or "D-new". See Decisions.
Damage
A case for a damaged item, with its own status machine from Reported to ReceivedByPartner. A damage the Partner bears debits their wallet up to the snapshotted suggested price times quantity. See Damage.
Dedicated website
A Partner's own public storefront (§16), fed by the ERP over the API and webhooks, counted against the package's Websites limit. See Website.
Delivery success fee
A percentage fee charged once against a Supplier's own payable when its order reaches Delivered; the rate is frozen on the row (delivery_success_fees). Not reversed by a later return. See Supplier.
DFCOMMERCE
The first external store platform with a driver (DfcommerceDriver). Also a design reference for the storefront, but none of its backend is reused (D30). See External store.
Deposit rule
A dated, scoped requirement for deposit and minimum balance (deposit_rules), resolved by scope precedence and never edited in place; a change closes one row and opens another (§24.1). See Wallet.
Distributed lock
App\Support\Concurrency\DistributedLock, backed by the isolated Redis lock database (connection locks). Also used by the scheduler's onOneServer().
Dropshipping / Partner Selling
A Partner publishes selected catalogue products on their website; Feriwala fulfils, so the Partner holds no stock. Feriwala is merchant of record (D12) except under Model B (D36).
Entitlement
What an account's package grants, read from a real subscription term (PackageFeature values such as staff_limit, website_limit). A missing feature row means zero, not unlimited. Enforced by the entitled middleware. See Package.
EPS
One of the eight payment gateways (EpsGateway), disabled in config by default; the guide PDF is the authority for its integration. See Billing.
Evidence slot
One piece of evidence a KYC requirement is made of: a file, image, PDF, typed value or declaration (KycRequirementSlot, D30 KYC). See KYC.
Exchange Order
The replacement order created at an exchange request (source = exchange), released to fulfilment only as inspection approves the returned goods. Numbered EX-{original order number}, then -2, -3 (D35).
External store
A partner's store on another platform, mirrored read-only (D29). No Feriwala payment, wallet or ledger row is ever written for its orders. Owner self-service connection was retired by D30. See External store.
Flat Taka
The money convention of D26: exact decimal Taka in NUMERIC(19,2), no poisha, nothing multiplied or divided by 100. See Database.
Fulfilment commitment
A Supplier's commitment to fulfil one order line from an on-demand or pre-order offer; the row is the capacity reservation, with a confirmation deadline swept hourly. See Supplier.
Gateway (payment gateway)
A payment provider behind the PaymentGateway contract: SSLCommerz, EPS, SurjoPay, AmarPay, bKash, Nagad, Stripe, PayPal. Credentials are encrypted rows, never env variables. See Integrations.
Grace period
A window after a package term ends or a balance falls short before services are restricted, configured per package or deposit rule (§8.4, §24.3). A stamped grace deadline is never moved by a repeat sweep.
HasPublicId / HasSlug
Model traits giving a ULID public_id or a unique slug for use in URLs. No database id may appear in a public URL.
Hold
Three distinct things: the wallet hold bucket (money set aside for a reason); an activation hold on an account (above); and an order hold, OnHold with reason partner_wallet_insufficient, for a Model B order whose receivable is not yet collectable (D36).
Idempotency key
A unique key making an externally triggered financial write safe to repeat, kept in a unique column (durable) and guarded by a Redis lock for the replay window. See IdempotencyService.
Identity status
User.identity_status: login-level suspension, blocking or closure, gating everything including administration (D23).
Inertia
The server-driven SPA layer (v3) used for the ERP pages in resources/js/pages; controllers return Inertia::render(). See Frontend.
KYC batch / round / version
A batch is a template of groups, requirements and slots; publishing freezes an immutable version; a round is one submission attempt, reading its structure from one version only (D30 KYC). See KYC.
Ledger entry
An immutable row in ledger_entries (or supplier_ledger_entries) recording every financial transaction with balance before and after. No update, no delete, enforced by trigger. Corrections are new rows (§23.2). See Wallet.
Locked column
A column a database trigger refuses to change after insert (feriwala_catalogue_columns_are_locked), such as public_id or an owner. See Database.
Low balance
A wallet below what its account must hold: the graded response is notify, grace, restrict, disable, auto-restore on top-up (§24.3). Also the account status LowWalletBalance and the website status of the same name.
Merchant of record
The party that takes the customer's payment and carries refund and chargeback duty. Feriwala for dropshipping Model A (D12); the Partner for Model B (D36).
Minimum balance / required deposit
Wallet amounts an account must keep (protected from withdrawal) and the initial deposit it must make, set by deposit rules (§24).
Mobile API
The Sanctum bearer-token, JSON-only API under /api/mobile/v1, self-scoped like the web ERP (§31.3) and reusing the same Actions and policies. See Mobile API.
Model A / Model B
The two payment directions for Store Orders: A, Feriwala's platform gateway (D12); B, the Partner's own gateway with a platform receivable recovered from the Partner wallet (D36). Frozen per payment as GatewayOwnership (Platform or Partner).
Money
App\Support\Money\Money: an exact decimal Taka string with bcmath arithmetic, explicit rounding, exact allocation and currency checking. Cast with MoneyCast. Parsed from input by DecimalAmount::parse().
Offer (Supplier offer)
One Supplier's independently priced, independently stocked claim on a product or variation (SupplierOffer). One variation may carry several. See Supplier.
Order source
Where an order came from, for example erp_wholesale, website, exchange. Fulfilment rules can differ by source (D13). See Order.
Package
An admin-created plan with a fee, validity, renewal terms, grace period and typed features (PackageFeature); an account's term is a user_packages row. See Package.
Partner
A business account that has been activated and trades, selling wholesale purchases or dropshipping through its website. The UI word for what the code calls a BusinessAccount. Distinct from a Supplier.
Partner gateway configuration
A versioned, encrypted, write-only store of a Partner's own gateway credentials for Model B; each payment freezes the exact version it used (D36). See Billing.
Partner stock
Units a Partner owns after an ERP wholesale purchase, in buckets (PartnerStockItem). A Storefront sells these or, for Non-Conditional accounts, platform stock, but the two are never mixed (D34). See Inventory.
Payable (Supplier payable)
What Feriwala owes a Supplier for one allocated order line. A claim, not money: it credits no wallet until settlement. Figures are locked by the database; a return appends a reversal. See Supplier.
Payout method
Where a Supplier or Partner is paid: one owner-polymorphic table, details encrypted, masked display, snapshotted onto a withdrawal at request time. See Payout.
Permission
A Spatie permission named <module>.<action> (for example withdrawal.approve), listed in PermissionCatalogue and seeded by RolesAndPermissionsSeeder. See Access.
Platform credit
The wallet kind that is spendable inside Banij but never withdrawable (top-ups, deposits, rewards). It becomes withdrawable only through an audited conversion; there is no reverse. A spend takes platform credit first.
Platform rate
What Feriwala charges for a Supplier-sourced item; the only figure a Partner ever sees. The Supplier Rate stays confidential (D25).
Platform receivable
What Banij is owed on a Model B Store Order, frozen at placement: product/platform recovery plus delivery plus delivery tax. Append-only; collected from the Partner wallet all-or-nothing (D36).
Platform role
One of the twenty-one Feriwala staff roles, not bound to a business account (D2, D23), as distinct from an account staff role such as Owner, Manager or Staff (AccountRole).
Poisha
One hundredth of a Taka. Deliberately absent from the system since D26; the word survives only in historical migrations and a few comments. A guard test fails the build if poisha conversion returns.
Proceeds (order proceeds settlement)
Whether and how much a Non-Conditional Partner earns on an order line (order_proceeds_settlements), credited only after delivery and confirmed collection. See Order.
Public ID
A ULID in public_id used wherever an identifier is exposed. The bigint primary key is internal only.
Reconciliation
Checking the system against an outside truth: gateway reconciliation (every two minutes), ledger integrity (daily), courier and COD reconciliation (§28). Reports a mismatch and repairs nothing silently. See Operations.
Reference (human reference)
A typed, unique code such as ORD-, PAY-, TXN-, WDR-, INV-, STL-, RFD-, RET-, DMG-, SHP-, generated by HasReference from ReferencePrefix.
Referral
The multi-level programme (D24): one direct referrer per account, commission to ancestors up to the plan's depth, off by default. See Referral.
Reservation (stock reservation)
Units set aside for an order not yet confirmed, expiring by a window (ReservationKind: online_payment or cod); the overselling guard. Released by a scheduled sweep every minute. See Inventory.
Retail margin
The Partner's selling price less the platform's entitlement. A calculation base for the earnings engine, not a money path of its own (D12). In Model B it is reporting only and never credited (D36).
Return charge
Half the original frozen delivery charge, rounded half away from zero and computed with bcmath, debited from the Partner at settlement after inspection approves (D35).
Reverb
Laravel's WebSocket server, used for the live notification bell and panel updates (php artisan reverb:start, port 8080).
Rotation grace window
How long a rotated website credential secret keeps verifying so a storefront can redeploy: STOREFRONT_ROTATION_GRACE_MINUTES, default 1440. See Website.
Row lock
PostgreSQL SELECT ... FOR UPDATE (lockForUpdate()) taken inside a transaction on the wallet, stock or order row. Required for every financial mutation.
Sanctum
Laravel's token authentication, used for the mobile API (personal_access_tokens).
Scope (credential scope)
A permission on a website credential: catalog:read, inventory:read, orders:read, orders:write, customers:write, returns:write (CredentialScope). Chosen explicitly, never defaulted (D32 P1).
Selection (website product)
A central product chosen for one storefront (WebsiteProduct). A selection, never a copy: the product's name, images and stock stay in the catalogue; the selection holds placement, display order, price and promotion within admin bounds (§15.1). See Website.
Self-scoping
The rule that a non-admin cannot reach another user's data through a URL, API call, export or modified parameter (§31.3). Implemented in queries and scopes, never only in the UI.
Settlement
The closing of a financial claim: gateway settlement of a payment, a return or damage case settlement after inspection, Partner proceeds settlement, Supplier payable settlement, and COD settlement against courier statements. Context decides which.
Shared address
The structured address book for business accounts and Suppliers, tied to bd_locations (division, district, upazila, union). Separate from free-text user_addresses (D28). See Address.
Slot (allocation slot / evidence slot)
In websites, one entitlement slot per permitted website, each with its own domain and hosting period (D30). In KYC, an evidence slot (above). Different tables, different meaning.
Source link
A legacy staff assertion that stock catalogued under a different product fulfils orders for this one (ProductSourceLink). No longer used by allocation; kept as inactive history. See Sourcing.
Sourcing group
A staff-declared set of catalogue products that can fulfil the same ordered product (ProductSourcingGroup), never inferred from names or SKUs. Some structures were later converted to product links and supplier assignments; see the module page.
SSLCommerz
The first and only default-enabled gateway (D7). Its sandbox cannot reach a local address, which is why a local payment may need the reconciliation sweep. Also the name of a (disabled) SMS provider entry.
Staff
Either Feriwala's own platform staff (platform roles) or an account's staff members under one owner, capped by the package Staff Limit (D1). Different scopes that never mix (D2).
Steadfast
The first real courier API behind the provider-neutral shipment domain (D8). Statuses arrive by webhook (Bearer token) and a five-minute poll through one locked action. "Delivered" never means "cash collected". See Courier.
Store ID / store password
The SSLCommerz credential pair a gateway configuration holds, either Feriwala's own or a Partner's under Model B. Held encrypted and masked; never copied into payment rows or logs.
Storefront
A customer-facing partner website application consuming the ERP's Storefront API. Since D32 each Owner has an independent installation built from the Master Storefront Template. See Storefront app.
Store Order
An order placed on a Partner's storefront, as against an ERP wholesale purchase. The unit Model B and Non-Conditional settlement apply to.
Supplier
A separate account domain (D25): its own model, supplier guard, KYC, portal, wallet and payables. Never convertible to or from a Partner login. See Supplier.
Supplier Rate
A Supplier's own price (supplier_rate), confidential to that Supplier and staff with supplier_pricing.view; never in a Partner prop, API response, export, log or notification (D25).
Supply mode
How an offer is supplied: ready_stock, on_demand or pre_order (SupplyMode). A declared fact, not a lifecycle, so "no confirmed stock" is never read as zero or unlimited.
Sweep
A scheduled, idempotent pass that moves things whose time has come (expiry, reconciliation, retries). All run with onOneServer() and withoutOverlapping(). See Operations.
Tax-exclusive
The default tax mode (D19): prices exclude tax and the tax is added; inclusive pricing is supported by configuration. No statutory rate is hardcoded. See Tax.
Taka
See BDT.
TransitionableState
Interface in App\Support\StateMachine that status enums implement, declaring transitionsTo(), label() and isTerminal(). Statuses move through transitionTo() (trait HasStateMachine), never by assigning the attribute.
Typed balance
The wallet split into platform credit and withdrawable money, with reserved and hold amounts remembering their split (.ai/rules/wallet.md). Never compute withdrawable as total - platform_credit.
Wallet
An account's set of balance buckets (total, reserved, pending, hold, required deposit, minimum balance, COD receivable) backed by the immutable ledger. Written only through WalletService. A Supplier has a separate supplier_wallets. See Wallet.
Wayfinder
Generates typed TypeScript route functions into resources/js/actions and resources/js/routes. Always regenerate with --with-form. See Commands.
Webhook secret
The signing secret for a webhook: a website's outbound endpoint secret, an external store's HMAC secret, or Steadfast's Bearer token. Shown once or encrypted, never logged. See Operations.
Website
A Partner's dedicated site record with its own status machine (WebsiteStatus, fourteen cases from SetupPending to Closed), domains, hosting, charges, credentials and webhook endpoint. See Website.
Wholesale
Bulk purchase by a logged-in Partner inside the ERP (order source erp_wholesale), always paid in full since D34. The public site never shows products or prices. See Wholesale.
Withdrawal
A Partner or Supplier request to pay out withdrawable wallet money, with default rules and per-user overrides, approval and a paid lifecycle. Blocked while a collectable Model B hold exists. See Withdrawal.
